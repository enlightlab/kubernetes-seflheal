How it works

  1. 1
    Deploy from chat

    Say deploy fastapi or deploy both apps. The agent registers GitOps and brings workloads up on the live cluster.

  2. 2
    Ask for status when you need it

    Say show status for a snapshot — FastAPI, Nginx, pods, and Argo CD sync in one view.

  3. 3
    Simulate a problem

    Say simulate outage to break staging safely, then what broke? for an AI root-cause explanation.

  4. 4
    Auto-fix and verify

    Say auto-fix to restore the good image, re-sync GitOps, and confirm the app is healthy again.
